Where can I spend time outdoors in Nobleton?

Nobleton Community Park, at 15 Old King Road, is a starting point for outdoor recreation. The Township lists a playground and sports amenities; check current access and seasonal availability before your visit.

Where is the Nobleton library?

The Nobleton branch of King Township Public Library is at 8 Sheardown Drive. Its official website provides current hours and library information.
